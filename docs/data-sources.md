# TerraSignal AI - Data Sources & Provenance Registry

Every major metric, valuation benchmark, and risk score in TerraSignal AI is grounded in verified public datasets and statutory registries.

---

## 1. Grounded Data Sources

### 1. National Housing Bank (NHB) RESIDEX
- **Authority:** National Housing Bank / Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (India)
- **Dataset:** Quarterly Housing Price Index (HPI) - City & Micro-Market Indices
- **Frequency:** Quarterly (Current: Q2-2026)
- **Coverage:** Chennai Metro & Suburbs (50+ Zones)
- **License:** Open Government Data (Data.gov.in)
- **Usage:** Benchmark for baseline price per square foot and historical quarter-over-quarter appreciation rates.

### 2. Tamil Nadu Real Estate Regulatory Authority (TNRERA)
- **Authority:** TNRERA Statutory Project Registry
- **Dataset:** Registered Residential Projects, Total Units, Unsold Inventory, Handover Timelines
- **Frequency:** Monthly Updates
- **Coverage:** CMDA Planning Area
- **Usage:** Tracks developer supply pressure, upcoming completions, and inventory absorption velocity.

### 3. Chennai Metropolitan Development Authority (CMDA)
- **Authority:** CMDA Master Plan 2026-2046 & Comprehensive Mobility Plan
- **Dataset:** Metro Phase 2 Corridors (3, 4, 5) Alignment Milestones and Peripheral Ring Road Progress
- **Frequency:** Bi-Annual
- **Usage:** Computes Infrastructure Grades (0–100) and transit premium forward catalysts.

### 4. State Disaster Management Authority & Water Resources Dept (WRD)
- **Authority:** Tamil Nadu WRD & Greater Chennai Corporation GIS
- **Dataset:** Micro-drainage Catchment Mapping, 100-Year Flood Line Inundation Hazard Survey
- **Frequency:** Annual
- **Usage:** Grounded environmental hazard ratings (Pallikaranai marsh basin, low-lying catchments vs. elevated corridors).

### 5. Reserve Bank of India (RBI)
- **Authority:** Reserve Bank of India Macroeconomic Statistics
- **Dataset:** Weighted Average Lending Rates (WALR) and Housing Credit Growth
- **Frequency:** Quarterly
- **Usage:** Mortgage interest rate sensitivity and repo rate shock modeling in the What-If Scenario Simulator.
